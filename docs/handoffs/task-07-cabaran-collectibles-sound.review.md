- **Medium — First touch can leave music silent.** [apps/web/src/audio/music.ts:40](C:/Users/yuiho/projects/pulau_kata/apps/web/src/audio/music.ts:40). With music files present, the first touch attempts playback on `pointerdown`, before [touch activation occurs](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/interaction.html#activation-triggering-input-event). Playback rejection is swallowed and the listener removed. Home → chapter → Kenali keeps the same track, so playback is never retried. **Fix:** unlock on `pointerup`/`click` and retain retries until playback succeeds.

- **Medium — Low volume and speech ducking fail on iOS.** [apps/web/src/audio/music.ts:109](C:/Users/yuiho/projects/pulau_kata/apps/web/src/audio/music.ts:109). All attenuation relies on `HTMLAudioElement.volume`, which [iOS ignores](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/AudioVideo/Conceptual/Using_HTML5_Audio_Video/Device-SpecificConsiderations/Device-SpecificConsiderations.html). Once music starts, it plays at full media volume and remains there during read-aloud. **Fix:** control music through a Web Audio `GainNode`; pause music during speech where gain control is unavailable.

- **Low — Muting does not stop an active jingle.** [apps/web/src/audio/sfx.ts:73](C:/Users/yuiho/projects/pulau_kata/apps/web/src/audio/sfx.ts:73). Once recorded jingles are supplied, switching sound off during the completion jingle leaves it playing for its remaining duration. The element is discarded, and `soundEnabled()` only guards new playback. **Fix:** retain active effect elements and stop them on sound-setting changes; also mute scheduled synthesized effects.
