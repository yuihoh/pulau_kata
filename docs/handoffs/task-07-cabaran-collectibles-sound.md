## Decisions worth challenging
1. **One sound switch** for music + effects (not two). Read-aloud ignores it.
2. **Collectibles are derived, not stored.** If a unit's content later gains a Misi, past players see it as locked until they play it.
3. **The Misi route stays under `lab`** (`#/lab/misi/<id>`) and is saved only when Uji is done.
4. **Music is a module-level singleton** that listens to `document` events. `setTrack` replaces the element (no crossfade between screens).
5. **The postcard is earned when all four stages are completed**, whatever the stars.
6. **Cabaran stars use first-try answers over all words** (unchanged from the prototype), so time-out and unreached words count against them.
7. **`gen-audio` uses REST with `fetch`** (no Azure SDK dependency) and a fixed pacing gap.

## Review outcome (Codex review: `task-07-cabaran-collectibles-sound.review.md`)
All 3 findings were valid (all about real-device audio behaviour) and are fixed. Regression tests are in `audio/audio.test.ts` (marked #n).

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium: the first touch could leave music silent (a `pointerdown` play attempt before activation was rejected, the listener was gone, and the same track never retried) | `MusicPlayer` listens on `pointerup`, `click`, `touchend` and `keydown`, and keeps listening **until `play()` actually resolves**. Before that, only a gesture may try to play. Test: a blocked first tap, then a click starts it |
| 2 | Medium: iOS ignores `audio.volume`, so music would play at full volume and never duck | Music goes through a Web Audio `MediaElementSource → GainNode` (`setTargetAtTime` fades: 0.25 normal, 0.06 ducked). Without Web Audio, music **pauses during speech** and resumes afterwards. `MusicPlayer` takes injectable `urlFor` / `audioContext` for tests. Tests: gain levels for normal/ducked/released; the fallback pauses and resumes; sound off pauses |
| 3 | Low: switching sound off didn't stop a recorded jingle already playing | `sfx.ts` tracks active jingle elements and routes synthesized sounds through a master gain; `stopAllSfx()` runs on switch-off. Test: a playing jingle is paused when sound is switched off |

Re-run: lint, format, typecheck, 159 web + 37 Worker unit tests, build, e2e 14/14.
